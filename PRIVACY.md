# Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Gopher Reader browser extension and the Gopher Client desktop app.

## What the extension handles

When you click the Gopher Reader button or press its shortcut, the extension reads the page in the current tab and converts its text and links into a reading copy. It does this only for the tab you choose, only when you ask.

The extension stores, inside your browser:

- the reading copy of each open reader tab, removed when that tab closes;
- your reader settings (font size, wrapping, appearance);
- bookmarks you create (page title, address and the time you saved it).

Form contents are never copied. Images are replaced by their text descriptions.

## What leaves the browser

Nothing is sent over the internet. The extension makes no network requests, has no analytics, and has no accounts.

If you click "Open in Gopher app", the reading copy is sent to the Gopher Client desktop app on the same computer, which saves it in your user folder and serves it on `127.0.0.1:7070`, a local address that is not reachable from your network or the internet. Saved copies stay on your computer until you remove them in the app.

## Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than showing and saving the pages you choose.
